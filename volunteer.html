Why Volunteer?

Be part of a people-driven campaign that puts Emabungo first. Whether you can give a few hours a week or more, your contribution matters.

  • Help organise community meetings and barazas
  • Support door-to-door and outreach activities
  • Assist with social media and content
  • Join event logistics and mobilisation teams
  • Share the manifesto and message of progress
Together, we build the future.

Your time and energy help turn vision into reality for Emabungo.
