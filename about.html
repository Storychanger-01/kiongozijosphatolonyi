A Leader Committed to Emabungo

Hon. Josphat Olonyi is a dedicated leader, advocate and public servant with a proven track record of empowering communities and building sustainable progress in Emabungo Ward and Vihiga County.

His life’s work is guided by integrity, compassion and a deep commitment to improving the lives of ordinary residents. He believes leadership is not about position — it is about people.
