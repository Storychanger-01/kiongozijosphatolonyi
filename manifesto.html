Our Agenda

Manifesto & Priorities

Practical solutions. Real impact. Community-driven change for Emabungo.

🎓 Education

Investing in quality education and skills to empower every learner.

  • Expand bursary support and school feeding programmes
  • Improve classroom infrastructure and learning materials
  • Promote digital literacy for teachers and students
  • Partner with institutions for vocational training
  • Support early childhood development centres

❤️ Health

Building accessible, affordable and quality healthcare for all.

  • Strengthen local dispensaries and health centres
  • Improve maternal, newborn and child health services
  • Expand community health outreach and preventive care
  • Advocate for better staffing and medical supplies
  • Promote mental health awareness and support

🛣️ Infrastructure

Developing modern infrastructure that connects communities and drives growth.

  • Rehabilitate and maintain key roads across Emabungo
  • Upgrade market infrastructure and trading centres
  • Improve street lighting and public safety
  • Advocate for better public transport and connectivity
  • Support drainage and flood-control measures

🏃 Youth & Sports

Empowering young people with skills, opportunities and sports.

  • Roll out youth skills and entrepreneurship programmes
  • Support local sports teams, tournaments and facilities
  • Create talent identification and mentorship platforms
  • Link youth to job and internship opportunities
  • Promote arts, culture and creative industries

🌾 Agriculture

Supporting farmers and promoting sustainable agriculture.

  • Improve access to quality inputs and extension services
  • Support value-addition and better market linkages
  • Promote climate-smart farming practices
  • Empower women and youth in agribusiness
  • Advocate for better storage and post-harvest handling

💧 Water

Ensuring clean, reliable and sustainable water for every community.

  • Rehabilitate and expand existing water points
  • Support community-managed water projects
  • Promote rainwater harvesting
  • Advocate for increased piped water coverage
  • Improve water quality monitoring and sanitation
